# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith, and remember that the
people opening issues here are often in the middle of an incident. Harassment,
discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@optimuslabs.io** with "leakpatrol conduct"
in the subject. Reports are handled
confidentially by the Optimus Labs maintainers.
